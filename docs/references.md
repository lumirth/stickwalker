# Credits

Stickwalker builds on [lumirth/pw](https://github.com/lumirth/pw), my matching reconstruction of the Pokéwalker firmware.

UnrealPowerz's [pw_firm](https://github.com/UnrealPowerz/pw_firm) inspired the reconstruction. h4lfheart's [PocketWalker](https://github.com/h4lfheart/PocketWalker) inspired the initial Pokéwalker work.

Dmitry Grinberg's [Pokéwalker research](https://dmitry.gr/?r=05.Projects&proj=28.%20pokewalker), Martin Korth's [GBATEK](https://problemkaputt.de/gbatek.htm#dscartinfraredpwalkerircommands), [PRET's HGSS reconstruction](https://github.com/pret/pokeheartgold) and mamba2410's [reverse-pokewalker](https://github.com/mamba2410/reverse-pokewalker) supplied research and references.

Thanks to the [Pokéwalker Hacking community](https://discord.gg/ymbTMsS), including mamba2410, zenithknight, mriankamp and porocyon.

The port uses [M5Stack's Stick S3 hardware and libraries](https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/StickS3). Firmware tests use [esp32sim](https://github.com/mikroverk/esp32sim), by Joakim Eriksson and Alice.
