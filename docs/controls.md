# Stick controls

A new Stick starts with **All three** and the left side down. An update keeps your saved layout and orientation.

| Button layout | Left | Center | Right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All three | Power | Front | Side |
| Power + Front | Power | Power + Front | Front |
| Front + Side | Front + Side | Front | Side |
| Power + Side | Power | Power + Side | Side |

Press Front or Side to wake the screen. Release the button before making your next move.

Hold Power alone for 1.5 seconds to open **Stick Settings**. Use Left and Right to move and Center to select. To change a mapping, open **Controls**, choose the buttons and their assignments, then select **Apply and close**.

**Orientation** rotates the screen. **Palette** selects one of the six palettes on the Stick. Edit their colors in [Stickwalker Desktop](../crates/sw-desktop/README.md). The game's **Shade** setting controls the backlight. **Sound** chooses how the speaker sounds: **Piezo** matches a real Pokéwalker's buzzer, **Piezo+** is the same tone 6 dB louder, and **Speaker** keeps the StickS3 speaker's fuller tone. The game's own volume setting still applies.
