# Take your Pokémon for a walk

Choose a Pokémon and a route in HeartGold or SoulSilver, transfer them to the
Stick, and take it with you. Walking earns Watts, which you can spend searching
for Pokémon and items along the route.

## Connect to your game

Start with [Stickwalker installed and the photodiode connected](install.md).
Use an infrared-equipped HeartGold or SoulSilver Game Card and a console that
can run it. Have a Pokémon in a PC Box and at least five free PC Box spaces.
If a Pokémon from this save is out on another walker, return it first.

1. Start the game and choose **Connect to Pokéwalker** from the main menu, then
   **Go for a Stroll**.
2. Choose a Pokémon from your PC Boxes and a route.
3. On the Stick, choose **Connect**. With the default buttons, M moves left,
   R moves right, and M+R together confirms.
4. Aim the Stick's infrared transmitter and photodiode at the Game Card's
   infrared window. Hold them still until both sides finish.

Your Pokémon will appear on the Stick with the chosen route. The transfer also
sets its clock and registers it with your game. Nintendo's
[connection instructions](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/7374/p/50/c/120)
show the game-side prompts.

## Walk and play

Carry the Stick while you walk. It counts steps with the screen off; hold M for
half a second to wake it, then release the buttons to navigate. Open **Poké
Radar** to search for Pokémon or **Dowsing Machine** to search for items. Both
use the same rules and Watts costs as the original Pokéwalker.

The [controls guide](controls.md) covers button layouts, screen rotation,
appearance and sound settings. Your Pokémon and progress are saved across
restarts.

## Bring your Pokémon and finds back

Open **Connect to Pokéwalker** in the game again. Choose **Return from a Stroll**
to bring your walking Pokémon back, or **Receive a gift** to collect your finds
while leaving that Pokémon on the Stick. Choose **Connect** on the Stick and
align its transmitter and photodiode with the Game Card again.

Return your Pokémon to the same game it came from. The
[HeartGold manual](https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/DS_Pokemon_HeartGold)
explains both transfer options.

## Connect with another walker

With a Pokémon on each walker, choose **Connect** on both and aim their infrared
transmitters and receivers at each other. Peer play records the encounter and
awards a gift. A repeat connection on the same day reports that you have already
connected. You can connect again after the walker's next day rollover.

## Connection trouble

For a timeout, check the [photodiode wiring](install.md#infrared-receiver), then
adjust alignment and distance. Keep both the Stick's transmitter and its
photodiode pointed at the other device throughout the transfer.

If an older Stickwalker says "cannot connect again" with a walker it has never
met, [update its firmware](install.md#update-an-existing-stickwalker-installation).
The October 10 update repairs the missing device ID on first boot and keeps
your Pokémon and progress. Update both Sticks if both run older firmware.
