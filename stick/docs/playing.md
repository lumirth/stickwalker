# Take your Pokémon for a walk

After [installing Stickwalker](install.md), connect the Stick to your HeartGold
or SoulSilver save, choose a Pokémon and a route, and take it with you.
Stickwalker runs the reconstructed Pokéwalker game: walking earns steps and
Watts, and those Watts let you search for Pokémon and items.

## Connect to your game

Use an infrared-equipped HeartGold or SoulSilver Game Card and a console that
can run it. Keep a Pokémon in a PC Box and leave at least five PC Box spaces
open. If your save already has a Pokémon out on another Pokéwalker, return it
before changing which walker you use.

1. Start HeartGold or SoulSilver and choose **Connect to Pokéwalker** from the
   main menu, then **Go for a Stroll**.
2. Choose a Pokémon from your PC Boxes and a route, following the game's prompts.
3. On the Stick, open the Pokéwalker menu and choose **Connect**. In the default
   button layout, M moves left, R moves right, and M+R together confirms.
4. Aim the Stick's infrared transmitter and receiver toward the Game Card's
   infrared window. Keep them aligned and still until both sides finish.

The transfer registers the walker, supplies the Pokémon and route data, and
sets the walker's clock from the console. Nintendo's
[connection instructions](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/7374/p/50/c/120)
explain the game-side prompts. If a connection times out, adjust the alignment
and separation before retrying.

## Walk and play

Carry the Stick while you walk. Step counting continues with the screen off.
Hold M for half a second to wake it, then release the buttons before navigating.
The [controls guide](controls.md) explains the other layouts and Stick Settings.

Use **Poké Radar** to find and catch Pokémon, or **Dowsing Machine** to search
for items. The reconstructed firmware keeps the original step-to-Watt rules,
encounter logic, battles, inventory and Pokémon interactions. Your progress,
Pokémon and registration are stored on the Stick and survive a restart.

The release uses placeholder art for built-in pictures and the font. Pokémon
and route artwork come from the game during transfer. For the original built-in
art, [make a local build using your own firmware image](../../assets/README.md).

## Bring your Pokémon and finds back

Open **Connect to Pokéwalker** in the game again. Choose **Return from a Stroll**
to bring your walking Pokémon back, or **Receive a gift** to collect your finds
while leaving that Pokémon on the Stick. Choose **Connect** on the Stick and
align the infrared windows again. The walking Pokémon returns to its registered
Game Card. Nintendo's
[HeartGold manual](https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/DS_Pokemon_HeartGold)
covers the two transfer options.

[App-only firmware updates](install.md#update-an-existing-stickwalker-installation)
keep your saved game data. Keep a full flash backup before updating.
